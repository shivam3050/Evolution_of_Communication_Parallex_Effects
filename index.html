Mobile Communication


0G (Pre-cellular):

Fault: No scalability, manual switching
Improvement: First mobile voice
Bandwidth: Very low
Technique: Analog radio
Latency: High
Use-case: Car phones

1G:

Fault: No encryption, poor quality
Improvement: Cellular concept
Bandwidth: Narrowband
Technique: Analog (AMPS)
Latency: Medium
Use-case: Voice calls

2G (GSM):

Fault: Very low data speed
Improvement: Digital communication, encryption
Bandwidth: ~9.6–14.4 kbps
Technique: TDMA / CDMA
Latency: High
Use-case: Voice, SMS

2.5G (GPRS):

Fault: Slow data
Improvement: Packet switching
Bandwidth: 50–114 kbps
Technique: GSM + packet
Latency: High
Use-case: MMS, WAP

2.75G (EDGE):

Fault: Limited speed
Improvement: Higher data rate
Bandwidth: ~200 kbps
Technique: 8-PSK
Latency: High
Use-case: Email, browsing

3G (UMTS):

Fault: Limited speed vs 4G
Improvement: Mobile data + voice integration
Bandwidth: ~384 kbps–2 Mbps
Technique: WCDMA
Latency: Medium (~100 ms)
Use-case: Video call, internet

3.5G (HSDPA):

Fault: Weak uplink
Improvement: Fast download
Bandwidth: Up to 14 Mbps
Technique: AMC
Latency: Medium
Use-case: Streaming

3.75G (HSPA+):

Fault: Limited vs LTE
Improvement: Near-4G speeds
Bandwidth: Up to 42 Mbps
Technique: MIMO
Latency: Lower
Use-case: Apps, video calls

4G (LTE):

Fault: High infra cost
Improvement: Full IP network, high speed
Bandwidth: ~100 Mbps
Technique: OFDMA + MIMO
Latency: Low (~50 ms)
Use-case: HD streaming, apps

4.5G (LTE-A):

Fault: Spectrum dependency
Improvement: Carrier aggregation
Bandwidth: 100 Mbps–1 Gbps
Technique: OFDMA + CA
Latency: Low (~30 ms)
Use-case: 4K, gaming

5G:

Fault: Coverage limits
Improvement: Ultra-low latency
Bandwidth: 1–10 Gbps
Technique: mmWave + Massive MIMO
Latency: ~1 ms
Use-case: IoT, AR/VR

6G (Concept):

Fault: Undefined
Improvement: AI-native
Bandwidth: >100 Gbps
Technique: Terahertz + AI
Latency: Near-zero
Use-case: Holographic, BCI
